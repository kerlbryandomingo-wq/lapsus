# Sophia privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Applies to Sophia for iOS, iPadOS and Android, developed by KRLBRYN.

**Sophia doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data.** It has no account system, no servers,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **Browsing history**, **downloads** and **saved Study Assistant notes** are stored only on your
  device. You can delete them at any time in Settings > Privacy and Security, or by deleting Sophia.
- **Cookies and website data** are kept by the system web engine so you stay signed in to sites.
  Clear them in Settings > Privacy and Security.
- **AI API keys** you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain on that device only. They aren't backed up
  to iCloud or sent anywhere except to the AI provider they belong to.

## Websites

Sites you open in Sophia, including your school portal, receive what any browser sends them. Their
own privacy policies apply. Sophia opens pages only over secure HTTPS connections and always checks
their certificates.

Sites can ask for the camera or microphone (for example an online class). Sophia asks you first for
every site, and iOS asks once more. You can remove a site's permission in Settings > Privacy and
Security > Website permissions.

## Study Assistant

The Study Assistant is off until you choose a provider. When you use it, Sophia shows you exactly
what will be sent, removes things that look like passwords, keys, email addresses or long ID
numbers, and sends that text only after you tap Send, directly to the provider you chose (OpenAI,
Anthropic or a service you configured), using your own account. That provider's privacy policy
applies to what you send. The offline demo sends nothing.

## Children

Sophia is intended for college students.
